About This Event

The 2025 Pyeongchang Alpine Slopestyle Open brings together the finest freestyle riders across South Korea and East Asia. Hosted on Phoenix Pyeongchang's legendary Olympic-standard terrain park, athletes will compete through a high-stakes obstacle course engineered with rail setups, technical gaps, and a signature jump line.

Competition Categories & Regulations

  • check_circle Open Men & Women (Ski / Snowboard): Open to all riders aged 16 and above with valid FIS or Korea Snowboard Association rider license.
  • check_circle Junior Division: Ages 12 to 15. Parental consent and mandatory hard-shell helmet with spinal protector required.
  • check_circle Judging Format: Best-of-two runs in Qualification heats; 3-run final round with top single score taking podium.
